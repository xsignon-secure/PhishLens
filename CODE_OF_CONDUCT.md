# Code of conduct

PhishLens follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good intent, give and accept
constructive feedback, and don't harass anyone. This applies in issues, pull
requests, discussions and any other project space.

To report unacceptable behaviour, use GitHub's
[private reporting](https://github.com/xsignon-secure/PhishLens/security/advisories/new)
or contact the maintainers of the
[xsignon-secure](https://github.com/xsignon-secure) organization. Reports are
handled confidentially.
