Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. What we collect

Account data: your email, company name, and a salted password hash. Scan data: domains you submit and the resulting posture scores. Operational data: audit events (logins, scans, changes) with IP addresses, retained for security.

2. What we don't do

No AI processing of your data. No sale of personal data. No third-party trackers or advertising cookies on this site. Scan results for your private domains are visible only to your tenant unless you explicitly share a report link.

3. How we use data

To operate the service, send the alerts you configured, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

4. Your rights

You can export your tenant's data at any time (Dashboard → Export) and request deletion by emailing info@xsignon.com.

5. Security

Passwords are bcrypt-hashed, sessions are short-lived signed tokens, API keys are stored as hashes, and tenant data is strictly isolated. No system is perfect — report issues to info@xsignon.com.

6. Changes

We will post updates here and note the revision date.
