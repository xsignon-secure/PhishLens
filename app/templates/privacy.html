Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. What we collect

Accounts: name, email, role, a password hash, sign-in times and the devices and IP addresses of active sessions, created by your company's administrator. Scan data: domains you submit and the resulting posture scores. Operational data: audit events (logins, scans, changes) with IP addresses, retained for security.

2. What we don't do

No AI processing of your data. No sale of personal data. No third-party trackers or advertising cookies on this site. Scan results for your private domains are visible only to your tenant unless you explicitly share a report link.

3. How we use data

To operate the service, send the alerts you configured, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

4. Your rights

You can export your tenant's data at any time (Dashboard → Export) and request deletion by emailing {% if contact_email %}{{ contact_email }}{% else %}GitHub issues{% endif %}.

5. Security

Passwords are stored as Argon2id hashes, owners and admins use an authenticator app, sessions are short-lived signed tokens, API keys are stored as hashes. No system is perfect — report vulnerabilities privately as described in our security policy.

6. Changes

We will post updates here and note the revision date.
